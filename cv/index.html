Work

  • 2021.08 - Present
    PhD Student, Graduate Research Assistant
    Theoretical Mechanobiology and Materials
    Conducting research in mechanobiology and materials under Dr. Vivek Shenoy.
    • Developed a physics-based theoretical model and applied Monte Carlo simulations to investigate cancer cell migration on viscoelastic substrates.
    • Analyzed experimental biological data and pharmacological interventions to quantify cancer cell migration dynamics.
    • Formulated a multiscale physics model integrating intracellular biological processes with tissue-level mechanics.
    • Engineered experiments for T cell activation, cytoskeletal imaging, traction force microscopy, and RNA sequencing analysis.
  • 2020.06 - 2021.05
    Graduate Research Assistant
    Biomechanics Lab
    Conducted research on wrinkling instabilities in tissue bi-layers.
    • Investigated wrinkling instabilities in tissue bi-layers using custom experimentally fabricated bio-inspired, fiber-reinforced composites.
    • Devised analytical models in ABAQUS to predict surface instabilities in bi-layer biological structures.
    • Developed a haptics-based platform for intravenous catheterization training.
  • 2019.01 - 2019.05
    Medical Device Intern
    Asian Institute of Gastroenterology
    Designed a novel, low-cost medical device for endoscopy instruments.
    • Developed a model prototype using SolidWorks and iterated for sensor integration compatibility.
    • Implemented a program to combine positional data with endoscopy image analysis to aid doctors in decision-making.
