👋 Hi there, I'm Vivek Sharma!

I'm a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering and Applied Mechanics at the University of Pennsylvania, working at the intersection of theoretical mechanobiology, biomechanics, and materials science. My research focuses on developing multi-scale models to understand biological processes, particularly in cell migration, immune response, and mechano-sensing.

🔬 **Research Interests:**
- **Mechanobiology & Biophysics** – Understanding how mechanical forces influence biological systems.
- **Computational Modeling** – Monte Carlo methods, molecular dynamics, and continuum mechanics.
- **Biomedical Applications** – Theoretical models for cancer metastasis, immune synapse formation, and medical device design.
- **AI & Machine Learning in Biology** – Developing deep learning models for cell segmentation, biophysical simulations, and pattern recognition in microscopy and sequencing data. Exploring physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) to enhance predictive modeling of cellular dynamics and ECM interactions. Using machine learning to analyze single-cell behavior, tumor microenvironments, and mechanotransduction pathways for improved biomedical insights.

📚 **Current Projects:**
- **Glassy Adhesion Dynamics in Cell Migration** - Cells don’t just crawl—they navigate complex, ever-changing environments. This study explores how cancer cells migrate on viscoelastic substrates, where time-dependent stress relaxation alters their movement. This work bridges cell mechanics and metastasis, offering new insights into cancer progression.
- **Mechano-Electro-Osmotic Model of Tumor Spheroids** - How do tumors generate internal stress and shape their cellular architecture? This work develops a mechano-electro-osmotic model to uncover the mechanisms driving solid stress and morphology changes in tumor spheroids.
- **Cell segmentation** -  I have explored 2D and 3D cell segmentation methods for single-cell analysis of tumor spheroids microscopy datasets, optimizing models for diverse imaging conditions.

🛠️ **Technical Skills:**
- **Modeling & Simulation** – COMSOL, ABAQUS, ANSYS, SolidWorks, AutoCAD
- **Programming & Data Science** – MATLAB, Python, C++, R, SQL, ML/AI for bio-image analysis
- **Experimental Methods** – Biomechanical testing, traction force microscopy, cell tracking

🌱 **Outside of Work & Hobbies:**
Outside of work, I enjoy reading, mentoring, travelling, being outdoors, staying active, trying new sports, and training for my lifelong dream of completing an Ironman triathlon.

📚 **Passion for Teaching:**
I’m passionate about education and mentorship. I find great joy in teaching complex concepts in accessible ways, and I regularly engage in opportunities to inspire the next generation of engineers. I strive to create an interactive, student-centered learning environment where students feel empowered to explore and learn. Check out my teaching page for more on this.
